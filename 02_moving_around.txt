STAY IN NORMAL MODE for all movement.
If text appears when you press these keys, press <Esc> then u to undo.

─── CHARACTER MOVEMENT ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  h   ← left      (think: h is leftmost on the home row)
  l   → right     (think: l is rightmost)
  j   ↓ down      (think: j has a tail that points down)
  k   ↑ up

Practice: use hjkl to reach the X below. No arrow keys!

  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .
  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .
  .  .  .  .  .  X  .  .  .  .  .  .
  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .
  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  .

─── WORD MOVEMENT ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[x]  w   jump to start of NEXT word      (Word forward)
[x]  b   jump to start of PREV word      (Back)
[x]  e   jump to END of current word

These are MUCH faster than holding l. Always prefer w/b/e over hjkl for words.

Practice on this line — jump word by word:
  the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog

─── LINE MOVEMENT ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[x]  0   go to the very START of the line  (zero)
[x]  $   go to the very END of the line
[x]  ^   go to the first non-blank character

Practice: put cursor anywhere on this line, then press $ then 0 repeatedly:
  →→→  [cursor here]  This is a practice line for 0 and $ movement  ←←←

─── FILE MOVEMENT ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[x]  gg  jump to the TOP of the file     (press g twice quickly)
[x]  G   jump to the BOTTOM of the file  (capital G)
[x]  {   jump up one paragraph/blank-line
[x]  }   jump down one paragraph/blank-line

Practice: press  G  to go to the bottom. Then press  gg  to come back.

─── COUNT + MOTION ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

You can PREFIX any motion with a NUMBER to repeat it:

  5j    move 5 lines down
  3w    jump 3 words forward
  2b    jump 2 words back
  10l   move 10 characters right

Practice: from the word START below, press  4w  to land on FOUR.
  START one two three FOUR five six

─── SCREEN SCROLLING ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[x]  Ctrl+d   scroll DOWN half a screen
[x]  Ctrl+u   scroll UP half a screen
[x]  Ctrl+f   scroll down Full screen
[x]  Ctrl+b   scroll Back full screen
[x]  zz       center the current line on screen (very handy!)

EXERCISE B: Word jumping
Put cursor on the first word below, then use only w/b/e to visit each word.

  apple banana cherry date elderberry fig grape

EXERCISE C: Jump around the file
  1. Press  G   — where did you land?
  2. Press  gg  — back to top?
